Today's ships rebuilt from cells

Each ship is her generated plan drawn by the real decks builder, with a look patched in on this page. Scale: 1 cell = 60 units of today's 200 × 300 drawing, both ways, so the freighter's three rooms across sit in her three cells and every room fits within 1.5 cells of its own. On the plan a cell is 140 × 100, so today's drawings come out 0.71 as tall for their width. In 3D a cell is 64 × 96, so the models come out 1.5 times as long. The cockpit is lit everywhere. The new fixture types are drawn by this page, not by ui/; their JSON shapes are in the cards at the bottom.
