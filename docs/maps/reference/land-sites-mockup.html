A site's rooms on the walked land

Since every solid world opened, you can set down anywhere and walk; the game's sites (huts, wrecks, camps) stand on the land as a front, a place like any other, with up to six roofed rooms behind it reached by compass doors. The game sends the rooms with no coords (area site.claim-hut), so the board has to place them itself. Today the board draws the front as a plain octagon, hides its doors, and goes blank when you step inside. Here: Pensilva's Claim Hut, the hut through the east door, the sample store through the north one. Top row: you've walked to the front and into the hut once, and stand at the front. Bottom row: you're inside the hut. The store hasn't been entered, so it isn't named or drawn, only its door shows (the front's own words say a store stands there).
