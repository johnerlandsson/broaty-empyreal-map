The board of octagons: where you are

Each panel is the same walk: 29 real places the game generates round Zennor, out of all three road ends: east along the Tinners' Track to a cairn, a knoll and a pond; south down the Terrace Track to the Terrace Road's end with a step off to a pond; down to the Carn; and west and north off Carn Bridge. You're at the cairn on the Tinners' Track, and you've clicked the pond off the Terrace Track, so the walked route there is lit. The plan is at its usual zoom, so the Carn and Carn Bridge walks are mostly off to the west. No symbols in the octagons: the background says what kind of ground each place is. The small folded corner is a note.
