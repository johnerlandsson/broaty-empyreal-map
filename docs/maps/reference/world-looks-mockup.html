A planet from high orbit, by its climate

The orbit scene's band as it's drawn today (the face's top, a rounder limb, by day), one world a column. Every option reads the same few facts: the star's colour (its light on the day side), the air's pressure (how much limb glow, none when airless), the albedo (how bright the ground), seas and ice as the game's own physics has them, and a world's veil where the game says one hides it. No cloud on any world: what the canopy says of cloud is weather, which changes and isn't sent.

Stars by their colour

One way, no pick needed unless you want changes: the six colours starColour gives, on the chart (the star and its glow), the lanes map (the dot), and as the light on a world's day side in the space scenes (which draw no star disc of their own).
