Over a port

Parked over Zennor (Tamsin, under orange Senara) and over Dolcoath's Pit (Orrin, ice, under the same star), by day and by night. Today the face is one flat ochre (Zennor) or frozen grey (the Pit) whatever the world. The town, the Pit and their lamps are as they are now in every option.

A giant from high orbit

Giants have no climate in the game, and their prose has bands ("Morvah's clouds … storms, bands"), so the bands stay. What changes is the light: today every giant is the same ochre whatever its star.
