Moving within a belt: three looks

Each scene uses the same decorative rocks. Screen up is out from the core, so rcs out always streams the rocks down and rcs in streams them up. A bearing across the belt streams them sideways, turned by how far it runs from straight out. (The alternative is bearing 0° always up. Those bearings mean nothing outside the belt, so I'd go with core-up.) The rocks never stand for real ones. The countdown goes in the deck band's corner card (below each), never in the scene. While closing on a rock there's no beam. The beam comes on once she stops beside it.
