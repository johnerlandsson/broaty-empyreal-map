Climbs and game trails on the walked land

John's own walk round Zennor, replayed from the logs: 693 places. The game has sent style since Nov 14: 19 walked ways come as road (each one we already draw as a track from the names), 2 as climb, both off the Saddle on the Downs, far south-east (one of them one-way, down from the Low Pass), and none yet as trail. You're standing on that Saddle, so its ways out are lit (it's out at the edge of what's walked, so the board is sparse here). The three ways marked “trail” here are pretend (plain ways near the Saddle) so you can see one; no trail has been walked. A way not taken that the game calls a climb gets the climb's look on its stub too (bottom-right of the Saddle). Close-in under each panel.
