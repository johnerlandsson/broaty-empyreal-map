The land coloured by the ground the game sends

John's own walk round Zennor, replayed from the logs: 664 places, 228 of them from Nov 14–15 with the game's terrain (stones, earth, dust, rock, ice, cushions, water; no woods, reeds, scrub or salt walked yet). The rest were walked before the game sent it, so they keep today's colour from the title (the fallback). Each panel is centred on the same place out on the Downs at the usual zoom. The game's eleven don't line up with our thirteen title groups: ours mix ground (rock, dust, water, salt, ice) with the place's shape (high, slope, low) and things on it (cairns, workings, road ends, lava). And one title can sit on different ground: "Low Pass" comes as stones 33 times and earth 16 times.
