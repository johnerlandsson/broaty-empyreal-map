Ships in 3D, closer: the picked styles

The four ships at about 640 × 420, the bells as cones and the collar ring banded yellow. Left: the band's camera (yaw 16, pitch 38), looking at her nose. Right: from astern and a little above (yaw 196, pitch 24), so the bells face you. FORE and AFT mark each end along her centreline. Under each ship, a measured check: every bell's throat against the hull path's aft face at its side, the boom against the nose, the ring against the collar's outer wall, and the hull's extent against the path's. The cockpit is lit.
