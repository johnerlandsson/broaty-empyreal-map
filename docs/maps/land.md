# The walked land

*Status: built. The board of octagons, routes, notes and forgetting, and Tamsin from above in the
top band. Last updated 2026-11-13. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-land-automap-design.md`;
the picked look: `reference/land-picked.png` (`land-picked-mockup.html`).*

## What it is and when it shows

Since empyreal b6728d0 you can walk out of Zennor onto Tamsin's generated land, 30 km round the
city. There is no plan of it in the game's data, so the map draws only the places you have walked,
as you walk them, and keeps them per character across logouts.

It shows whenever the location's area starts with `surface.` (today only `surface.tamsin`;
`ui/lib/land-choose.js`, `landShown`). Both bands are the land's then: the station models, decks,
ships and cars are hidden, and the info line gives the place's name as the game sends it ("Old
Shaft on the Terrace Track"). Stepping back into Zennor brings Zennor's skyline and plan back. The
chart, the lanes map and the belt map still cover the top band as they do anywhere.

Only walked places are ever drawn or named. A way you haven't taken is a stub, and where it goes is
never named. No height, distance in km or other number about the ground is shown; the only number
is a route's "N steps the way you know".

## Where its data comes from

- **The game.** Each `mudstd.room.info` frame in a `surface.*` area is posted by the Lua side
  (`src/station_map/land.lua`, `src/main.lua`) as a `land-room` message after `location`: `id`,
  `name`, `area`, `areaName`, `coords` (km east `x` and north `y` of Zennor's point, height `z`)
  and `exits` (`{ id, area?, inverse? }` by compass point). An exit into Zennor carries
  `area: "zennor"`. The game sends no chart of the land and never names a place you haven't
  walked.
- **The kind of ground** isn't sent as data: it's in the name, which the game builds from a fixed
  list (`placeTitle()` in empyreal `surface-words.ts`). `ui/lib/land-kinds.js` maps a name to one
  of eleven ground groups (open, waymark, high, slope, low, rock, dust, workings, water, salt,
  ice), and the road ends (`….join.…` ids) are their own group, road. "on the X Track" puts a
  place on track X; "on the Downs", "by the Carn" give its region.
- **Zennor's block and roads out** come from `ui/data/land.js`, generated by `npm run build:data`
  from `content/game/land.yaml`: the port it's measured `from`, the `town` outline in km and each
  road end's spot and Zennor room (`joins`). Nothing else of land.yaml is written, since its
  heights, valleys and tracks would show land you haven't walked. A world with land but no entry
  still draws its walked places, with no town (`landWorld`).
- **The globe** is Tamsin's face (`faceOf("tamsin")`, `ui/lib/worlds.js`) turned to Zennor's
  latitude from `ui/data/sky.js`.

## What's kept

`ui/lib/land-store.js` (`LandStore`) keeps the walked places: each one's `name`, `x`, `y`, `z`,
exits and `note`, in the order first walked (which decides who wins when two places round to one
octagon, so a reload gives the same board). Routes run on a copy of the Mallard automapper's graph
and route finder in `ui/lib/automap/` (see its README for the commit and our changes).

The panel posts the store's record as `land-save` `{ who, record }`, debounced 1 s, and never while
no character is known; the waiting save is also flushed when the panel is hidden or closed. The Lua
side writes it under `land:<who>` in Mallard's storage, for whichever character it's sent for (the
panel only ever sends a character's own map).

Lua keeps whose map the panel has (`land_who`, apart from the lanes' character). When `Char.Status`
names someone else, or anyone at all after a disconnect, Lua sends her map as `land-load`
`{ who, record }`, then the last land frame again: on login the room frame comes before the status,
so it was walked into no one's map. The panel first saves the one before's waiting change under her
name, then loads; places walked while no one's map was loaded are merged in, the newer winning. A
disconnect sends `land-load` with no `who`: the panel saves what's waiting and clears the map, its
notes and where you are. When the panel opens, Lua replays the map's `land-load` and the last frame.
A missing or bad stored value loads as an empty map.

## Drawing the board

`ui/land-deck.js` draws the bottom band; `ui/lib/land-layout.js` works out the layout.

- **The tiling.** Regular octagons, 1.2 km flat to flat, faces to the eight compass points, on a
  1.2 km square tiling centred on Zennor's point. A place takes the octagon its spot rounds to; if
  that's taken, the later one takes the nearest free octagon.
- **Ground.** Each octagon has its group's colour with its texture over it, in screen pixels, so
  the texture is the same size at any zoom. The colours are in the style guide's §4.2 ("On the
  land's map").
- **Ways.** Side by side, a way is an Earth doorway across the shared face; diagonally, an Earth
  line across the corner square where four octagons meet. When the game's exit names a different
  compass point from the neighbour's octagon, the way leaves by the face the exit names and bends
  round to the face it arrives by, so the map never shows a wrong direction to type. A way to a
  place further off runs over open ground. A one-way way (down a scree) gets a small Fixture-line
  chevron pointing the way you can go.
- **Tracks** are drawn as Zennor's roads: an Earth band with a dashed Stone edge, named in Dim
  italic along the walked track. Region names sit in Dim italic among their walked places.
- **Zennor** is a dark block with a dashed Stone edge and "ZENNOR", with an Earth road to each road
  end you've walked. An exit into Zennor is that road, never a stub.
- **Ways not taken** are short dotted Dim stubs out of the face they leave by.
- **Your place** glows amber, as on every plan. A place with a note has a small folded corner.
- **No hachures.** Heights come to 0.1 km, too coarse to tell a climb from level ground; the high,
  slope and low backgrounds carry the lie of the land.
- **Chrome.** `N ▲`, + − ⌂, the wheel and drag as on every plan. It starts centred on you at about
  26 px a km and re-centres only when you walk out of the middle of the view; ⌂ centres on you.

## Finding your way

- **Click a walked place** to light the shortest walked route to it from where you are: a dashed
  amber line with no glow, a dashed amber outline on the place, and a card with its name and "N
  steps the way you know". Click it again, click empty ground or press Esc to clear. Walking on
  keeps the target and redraws the route, until you arrive.
- **The way home.** When Zennor is wholly off the view, a "← Zennor" card sits on the edge toward
  it; clicking it lights the route to the nearest walked road end.
- **Hover** a walked place for its name and note.

## Notes and forgetting

Right-click a walked place for **Note…** and **Remove note**. Right-click empty ground for
**Forget this land…**, which asks once, then clears this character's map. Notes are kept with the
map.

## The top band

`ui/land-globe.js`: Tamsin from above, a whole low-poly globe on the seeded stars in its own
colours, lit from a fixed light at the upper left with no day and night. Your spot is amber with
its ring and the glow, a little north of the middle at Zennor's latitude, moved by your `coords`
(at most 30 km, so in practice it's Zennor's spot). No labels and no weather.

## Wiring

`ui/main.js` builds one `LandStore`, and the `LandDeck` and `LandGlobe` on the first land location.
`land-room` goes into the store, then the board redraws and follows you and the globe moves your
spot; `land-load` loads the store and redraws. `render()` takes the land branch before the
stations': it hides the station model and every deck, shows the land's views and sets the info
line; leaving the land hides them again.

## Keeping up with the game

- `ui/lib/land-kinds.test.js` ("the game's titles"), run when `~/dev/empyreal` is there, collects
  every title `placeTitle` can return and fails if one isn't in `land-kinds.js`'s table. See
  [sync.md](sync.md) for what to do then.
- Land on a new world (`content/game/land.yaml`) is an area `surface.<world>`, which maps with no
  change; `build:data` adds its `town` and `joins` to `ui/data/land.js`.
- Updating the copied engine: `ui/lib/automap/README.md`.
