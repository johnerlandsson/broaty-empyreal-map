# Orrery Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A ◎ toggle on the top band switches it to a live, true-scale chart of the star system you're in, drawn from `Space.Charts` and `Ship.Status`, showing only what the player knows.

**Architecture:** A new Lua reducer (`src/station_map/charts.lua`) cleans `Space.Charts`, and folds `Ship.Status` into the ship's chart state. That state includes the place she left (`from`), the point she stopped at, whether she's searching for a belt, and when the status arrived. `src/main.lua` posts both to the panel, and keeps the toggle's on/off for the session. In the panel, all the maths is pure and tested in `ui/lib/orbits.js`: Kepler, positions, belt error, the ship's leg, framing and edge markers. `ui/chart-view.js` draws it as SVG over the top band once a second, and `ui/main.js` wires it in.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules and SVG in the panel, vitest for pure JS, Lua 5.4 with the repo's `tests/support/t.lua`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-orrery-design.md`, following `docs/maps/style-guide.md`. The approved look is option B in `docs/maps/reference/orrery-mockup.html`.

## Global Constraints

- **Only what the player knows.** Never draw a search leg, never narrow a fit's error, never place a mouth without `edge`, and never place a ship that can't be worked out from `from`, a known `location`, or a remembered point.
- **True scale only:** one km-per-px for the whole chart, with no compressed radii.
- **Amber `#ffb347`** is only for your ship, her route, her destination ring, and the arc of a belt she's searching for. The ship and the searched arc pulse (opacity 1 → .55 → 1, 1.8 s), with a light stroke `#fff3df` and the glow filter.
- **Colours:**
  - Orbits: `#2b3646`, 1 px.
  - Star: `#fffaf0` with a radial glow, at least 5 px.
  - Planets: Tones `#3a4556` / `#7e8da3` (lit half toward the star), Model edge `#0d1218`, at least 3.5 px.
  - Stations: a Glass `#cfe3ff` diamond.
  - Belts: Tone `#7e8da3`.
  - Mouths: a double chevron in Glass.
  - Labels: Dim `#7d8a9a`, 10 px, Inter.
- **Time:** wall-clock ms from `Date.now()`. Mean anomaly is `phase + 360 * (t - epoch) / 1000 / period` degrees, and angles are counterclockwise from +x.
- **Belt error after `at`:** `error × ((t − seen)/(at − seen))^1.5`, capped at π·a. Use the sent error unchanged when `t ≤ at` or `at − seen < 600000`. A belt is lost when its error is ≥ 0.9·π·a.
- **The edge:** `EDGE_KM = 15000`, from `content/help/lanes.md` in `~/dev/empyreal`.
- **Unplaced mouth label:** `<Star> lane, somewhere on the edge`, where `<Star>` comes from the mouth's name (the payload's `"the Maelor lane mouth"` gives `Maelor`).
- **Folding:** a satellite whose orbit is under 8 px across folds into its parent, unless it's the ship's place or destination.
- **Zoom limits:** 1,000 km wide at the closest, and 2.3 × the system radius wide at the furthest.
- **Follow margin:** 0.35, as on the plans.
- **Redraw rate:** at least once a second while the chart is shown and the panel is visible; never while hidden.
- **Bad data never throws** (style guide §10).
- **The chart's on/off:** kept by Lua for the session. It isn't a Mallard setting.

## Review Focus

- **A `Space.Charts` with odd entries** (a body with no orbit and not a star, an orbit with `period: 0`, a parent missing from the payload, a place with `orbit` a string): those items have no position and aren't drawn, and everything else draws. Pinned in Task 1's `cleans` tests and Task 3's `positionOf` tests.
- **The panel opening mid-trip** (no `from`): no ship and no route, but the destination ring is drawn. Pinned in Task 1 (`first status already under way has no from`) and Task 4 (`shipFix … bound`).
- **Crossing into Maelor's system:** the chart switches to the new system, reframes, and drops `from`, the point and searching. Pinned in Task 1 (`a new system drops from, point and searching`) and checked in Task 7's preview.
- **`progress` 1 or `eta` 0 on the last status of a trip:** no division by zero, and she's at the destination. Pinned in Task 4 (`legOf with progress 1`).
- **A search given up, or a sweep:** the arc stays lit, and no chevron is drawn. Pinned in Task 1's searching tests and Task 4's `shipFix searching`.

### Task 3: Orbit positions (`ui/lib/orbits.js`)

**Files:**

  - `offsetOn(orbit, epoch, t) -> [x, y]`: km from the parent.
  - `parsePoint(id) -> {body, x, y} | null`.
  - `parentOf(index, id) -> id | null`.
  - `positionOf(index, id, t) -> [x, y] | null`: km from the star.
  - `frameOf(index, a, b) -> id | null`: the nearest body both orbit, directly or not.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** The fixture is Senara's system from `system.yaml` (senara star; tamsin a 6000 period 259200; tiss a 900 period 7200 parent tamsin; orrin a 12000 e .1 phase 150 period 733137; orrin_station a 800 parent orrin; gannet belt a 2700 parent tamsin; mouth with and without `edge`), with `epoch` 0.
  - `circular quarter turn`: `offsetOn({parent:'tamsin', a:900, e:0, peri:0, phase:0, period:7200}, 0, 1800000)` ≈ `[0, 900]` (toBeCloseTo, 6 digits).
  - `eccentric at periapsis and apoapsis`: `{a:12000, e:.1, peri:30, phase:0}` at t=0 gives `10800·[cos30°, sin30°]`. At phase 180 it gives `13200·[cos210°, sin210°]`.
  - `nested`: `positionOf(i,'tiss',t)` equals tamsin's position plus tiss's offset.
  - `star at origin`: `positionOf(i,'senara',t)` is `[0,0]`.
  - `point`: `positionOf(i,'pt:tamsin:1500:-2000',t)` is tamsin's position plus `[1500,-2000]`. `parsePoint('pt:tamsin:x:1')` is null.
  - `mouth`: with `edge {r:15000, bearing:90}` it's `[0,15000]`. Without it, null.
  - `unknown or cyclic parent is null`.
  - `frameOf`: `('tiss','gannet')` → `'tamsin'`, `('tiss','orrin_station')` → `'senara'`, `('pt:tamsin:1:1','tiss')` → `'tamsin'`, and an unknown id → null.

- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx vitest run ui/lib/orbits.test.js`. Expected: FAIL (no module).

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement it.** Solve Kepler with Newton iterations from E = M (8 are plenty for e ≤ 0.3). The true anomaly is `2·atan2(√(1+e)·sin(E/2), √(1−e)·cos(E/2))`, `r = a(1 − e·cosE)`, and the angle is `ν + peri`. `positionOf` recurses with a depth guard of 8.

- [ ] **Step 4: Run** it again. Expected: PASS.

### Task 5: Framing and marks (`ui/lib/orbits.js`)

**Files:**
- Modify: `ui/lib/orbits.js`
- Modify: `ui/lib/orbits.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 3 and 4.
- Produces:
  - `systemRadius(index) -> km`: `max(edge.r)` or `EDGE_KM` if the system has any mouth, else the furthest apoapsis from the star, summed through parents.
  - `homeBox(index, ship, t) -> [minX, minY, maxX, maxY]` in km:
    - moving: the leg's two ends and her, padded 15%;
    - at a place, or searching: the place's parent, out to its furthest known satellite's apoapsis × 1.15, or the whole system if the parent is the star;
    - otherwise: ±1.08 × `systemRadius`.
  - `fitBox(box, w, h) -> [x, y, vw, vh]`: an SVG viewBox in km with y flipped (svg y = −km y), matching the w:h aspect, at least 1000 wide.
  - `edgeMarker(vb, p, inset) -> {x, y, angle} | null`: `vb` and `p` are in viewBox units. Null if `p` is inside `vb`; else the point on `vb` shrunk by `inset` along the ray from its centre to `p`.
  - `follows(vb, p, margin = 0.35) -> bool`: true when `p` is further from the centre than `margin` of the half-width or half-height.
  - `folded(index, id, kmPerPx) -> bool`: the orbit's `2a / kmPerPx < 8`.
  - `scaleKm(kmPerPx, maxPx) -> km`: the largest of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ that fits in `maxPx`.
  - `ago(ms) -> string`: `"2d 4h"`, `"3h 2m"` or `"12m"`, in the game's `charts` style.
  - `mouthStar(name) -> string`: `"the Maelor lane mouth"` → `"Maelor"`, falling back to the name itself.
